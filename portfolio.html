Fleet management for mixed AMRs with a Unity digital twin

Final-year project, CNIMI laboratory, UQTR, Québec, Canada, 2026

One system that coordinates three different robots (MiR250, MiR600 and Omron LD-90) from a single task interface, with a live 3D twin to watch and test missions.

  • Wrote one driver per robot brand: MiR over rosbridge WebSocket and REST, Omron over its ARCL protocol, so every robot looks the same to the fleet layer.
  • Orchestrated the fleet with Open-RMF and built a custom task client for goto, patrol, loop, delivery and charge missions.
  • Connected a Unity digital twin to ROS 2, with direct control of one robot or fleet-wide dispatch.
  • Migrated the MiR software stack from ROS 1 to ROS 2 Jazzy and chose Unity after comparing Gazebo, Isaac Sim, Unity and Unreal Engine.

ROS 2 Jazzy, Open-RMF, Nav2, Unity, Python, Ubuntu 24.04

Source code on GitHub
